# ShopEase Shipping and Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 August 2026

## 1. Shipping Coverage

ShopEase ships orders across India and to selected international destinations. International shipping availability depends on the destination country.

## 2. Delivery Timelines

| Shipping Method   | Estimated Delivery Time |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Standard Delivery | 3–7 business days       |
| Express Delivery  | 1–2 business days       |
| Remote Areas      | Up to 10 business days  |

Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary due to weather conditions, transportation disruptions, or public holidays.

## 3. Order Processing

Orders are generally processed within **24 hours** of successful payment confirmation. Orders placed after 6:00 PM may be processed on the next business day.

## 4. Address Changes

Customers may request a delivery address change **before the order is shipped**. Once the order has been shipped, address changes are not available.

## 5. Delivery Attempts

Delivery partners may attempt delivery up to **three times**. If delivery fails after three attempts, the order may be returned to the ShopEase warehouse.

## 6. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders with a value of **less than ₹10,000**. ShopEase may disable COD for certain postal codes or high-risk orders.

## 7. Tracking

Customers receive a tracking number by email or SMS once the order is shipped. Tracking updates may take up to **12 hours** to appear after dispatch.

## 8. Delayed Deliveries

If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery period, customers may contact ShopEase Support for assistance.
