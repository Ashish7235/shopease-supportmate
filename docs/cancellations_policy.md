# ShopEase Order Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 August 2026

## 1. Cancellation Before Shipment

Customers may cancel an order **before it is shipped** through the ShopEase website or mobile application. Approved cancellations stop the order from being dispatched.

## 2. Cancellation After Shipment

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled. Customers may use the return process after delivery if the product is eligible for return under the Returns and Refunds Policy.

## 3. Refund for Cancelled Orders

For prepaid orders cancelled before shipment, refunds are initiated within **5–7 business days**. The refund is processed to the original payment method.

## 4. Cash on Delivery Orders

COD orders cancelled before shipment do not require refund processing because no payment has been collected.

## 5. Partial Cancellation

If an order contains multiple items, customers may cancel individual items before shipment, provided those items have not been packed or dispatched.

## 6. Seller-Initiated Cancellation

ShopEase may cancel an order if:

* The product is out of stock.
* Payment verification fails.
* The shipping address is incomplete.
* Fraudulent activity is suspected.

In such cases, prepaid customers receive a full refund.

## 7. Cancellation Confirmation

Customers receive an email confirmation once the cancellation request has been approved.
