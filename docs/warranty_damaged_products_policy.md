# ShopEase Warranty and Damaged Products Policy

**Effective Date:** 1 August 2026

## 1. Warranty Coverage

ShopEase provides a limited warranty on selected products sold through the platform.

| Product Type | Warranty Period |
| ------------ | --------------- |
| Electronics  | 12 months       |
| Accessories  | 6 months        |

The warranty period starts from the date of delivery.

## 2. What Is Covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects, hardware malfunctions during normal use, and defects present at the time of delivery.

## 3. What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

* Physical damage caused by the customer
* Liquid damage
* Damage caused by unauthorized repairs
* Normal wear and tear
* Loss of accessories

## 4. Damaged-on-Arrival Products

Products that arrive damaged must be reported within **48 hours of delivery**. Customers should provide:

* Order number
* Photos of the product
* Photos of the package
* A brief description of the damage

## 5. Resolution Process

After verification, ShopEase may offer:

* Replacement of the product,
* Repair through an authorized service center, or
* Refund if replacement stock is unavailable.

Replacement is generally preferred when stock is available.

## 6. Warranty Claim Process

To submit a warranty claim, customers must contact ShopEase Support and provide proof of purchase. Additional diagnostic information may be requested for electronic products.

## 7. Inspection Timeline

Warranty inspections usually take **3–5 business days** after the product reaches the service center.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

ShopEase is not responsible for indirect losses such as loss of data, loss of business, or consequential damages arising from product failure.
